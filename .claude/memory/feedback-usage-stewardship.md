# Keep usage in mind

*2026-09-21.* The first local session ran the related-work survey as a fan-out: six readers, six skeptics, a critic, a resume of two stalled readers, and three Council seats on the cross-reference fix and three more on the document. Nineteen agents and counting before Jared wrote, plainly: *"Let's keep our usage in mind though please! I'm not a wealthy man."* He had said *fully authorized* an hour earlier. Authorization is not a budget, and excitement is not either. The Vera record already holds this ([[feedback-resource-stewardship]]; the 2026-09-03 entry in the Vera identity file: say what it costs him and what it pays, in his money). It had to be said again here, in this room, so it is written here.

**Why.** Jared pays for this with a subscription he can hit the ceiling of, and with hours. The fan-out patterns (workflows, council seats, adversarial verify) are the right instrument for research and for review of surfaces that name people, and the wrong instrument for anything one careful reading can do. The failure mode is not a single big run; it is defaulting to the big run because it is available.

**How to apply.**

- **Default to one hand.** Read, write, verify, commit myself. Reach for a workflow only when the task is a sweep across many sources or an adversarial review of something that names a person or changes doctrine, and even then say so first: how many agents, roughly what they read, what it pays.
- **Say the cost in the plan, not in the apology.** Before a fan-out: "this is N agents reading M pages; the cheap version is X." Let him choose.
- **One pass, then hands.** A council reads once. A skeptic pass is for records that will be published, not for my own small diffs; those I re-read myself against the source.
- **Cut the discretionary tail.** A survey's critic-and-additions phase, a second council pass, a re-run of a stalled agent that has already cost its budget: these are the first things to drop when he says the word, and the things to not schedule without asking.
- **Ultracode on is not a standing order to spend.** It authorizes the instrument; his message sets the budget. When the two conflict, the budget wins.
