# Council reading: a person's year, drawn

*Held 2026-09-27, on PR #73 at `3483aa7`: the year figure on every officeholder page, which replaced
the per-Signal strip of squares and the annual mini-figure in the answer, with the folds and the
Signal pages' rule drawings that came with it. It was read over the landing-state scratch build (the
annual Signal published, 33 Findings), at 1280 and 390 pixels wide, in light and dark. Prompt blob
`18ddca5a8f05b484d2a74ca1ba49a41d0299c549`. Four seats sat, alone and cold: A, B, D (twice, one pass
for each side, the side that went second at the last reading going first) and E. A page is the
window and not the record, so the seats were asked for what would make the figure false, misleading
or unfair about a named person, or unusable for its reader, and not for taste. Every seat checked the
figure against the rows: on all 27 transaction-report Findings the bar is the largest number of days
past a deadline and the ticks are the distinct deadlines passed; the figure's counts match the
answer's on 439 of 439 pages; one line of time and one order on every page. Names and document ids
stayed with the maintainer; this record carries neither.*

## Blocking, and changed

- **The day after the latest date was drawn as "in between" (Seats A, B, D and E).** Three annual
  reports are dated the day after the latest date an extension could reach, which the Signal does not
  evaluate. The figure gave every report it did not evaluate the pale mark of the time an extension
  may cover, and its label said so, while the sentence above said *the day after*. The figure's own
  docstring promised it could not disagree with that sentence. **Changed:** a fourth mark, an
  outline, for a report the Signal did not evaluate for any reason but the window, with the run
  record's own words for the reason in its label; the key names it.
- **Documents the register could not read were left out of the annual lane (Seats A, B and D).** On
  25 pages the sentence says a document attributed here printed no header it could read, and the
  figure drew an empty lane that read *no annual report attributed here*. Two pages with the same
  dates and the same index code drew one mark and one empty box, according to whether a header was
  legible. **Changed:** each such document is an outlined mark at the date the index gives it, and
  the caption says what is drawn and what is listed instead.
- **2026 was drawn blank (Seat E).** The line of time runs to the build's month and the register
  reads only the 2025 index, so a heavy trader's reports stopped in December and nine empty months
  read as trading that stopped. **Changed:** the part after the last day the index lists a report is
  outlined across the lane and says *the Clerk's 2026 index: not read in this build*.
- **"It does not show whether any trade was allowed" (Seats B and D).** The Act asks for reports and
  prohibits none of these trades; under a person's trades the sentence raised a question the law does
  not, and it travelled with a crop. **Changed:** *The law asks for the reports and does not prohibit
  the trades. It shows dates.*

## Not blocking, and changed anyway

- An annual Finding had no bar for its days past while a transaction Finding did, and the two
  Signals' Findings fall differently across the divide (Seat D). Both carry the bar now.
- A dot is a date, not a trade (Seat E): 7,346 rows draw as 1,314 dots. The key says *one or more
  trades on a date*; a row the filer marked Deleted is not drawn.
- The line from a report's trades to its date and the bar's start were unkeyed (Seat B). Both are in
  the key: *dates only*, and *from the earliest deadline the report's date passed; each tick is one
  deadline*.
- Lane words ran off the drawing (Seat E). They sit inside the outline where they fit and before it
  where they do not.
- The line of time stretched to each page's own latest date, so the same days drew at different
  lengths on different pages in a build made before August (Seat A). One line for the whole build.
- No mark said when service began (Seats A and E). A swearing-in mark where it falls after the
  Congress began; a member sworn in after the year is told so in the annual lane's words.
- The key drew the adverse annual mark pale (Seat A), because an older rule for the Findings'
  figures matched it. Fixed.
- *This is not a ruling by anyone* sat below the drawing, a phone's height from the sentence it is
  about (Seat B). It sits under the sentences now, before the links.
- At 390 pixels the drawing's words were about 6 pixels (Seat E). The drawing keeps a legible width
  and scrolls sideways inside its frame; the page does not.
- Thirty labels ended "one line of time. ." (Seats D and E). Each empty lane's words are in the label.
- A panel said *on every figure, the notice is a diamond*, and the year figure draws no notice (Seat
  E). *Wherever a figure draws it.*

## Not taken

- **When a page change publishes (Seat D).** Every Finding's page gains a drawing weeks before a
  general election, and no written rule says when a page change publishes. It publishes when the
  maintainer merges it, like every change to the pages since the founding; writing that rule down
  belongs with the METHODOLOGY cadence item in NEXT.md D.4, which already asks for it for builds.
- **A departed member's page (Seat B).** No row in this build has an office that ended, so the
  figure for one was not read. The figure takes the page's rows as they stand; the next reading of
  a departure should look at it.
